HOW TO PLAY

PCBL DRAFT

  1. Draft 12 cards, choosing one from three options every pick.
  2. Your final roster must contain exactly 7 hitters and 5 pitchers.
  3. Your main ratings are Average FB, Average BRK, and Average OFF.
  4. Earn bonus points for Season Diversity, Series Diversity, and your Rating Total.
  5. Rarity is calculated from each card's overall rating: Common = bottom 50%, Rare = 50–70%, Epic = 70–90%, Legendary = top 10%.
  6. When the draft is complete, you can take the exact same 7H / 5P roster into singleplayer or online ATTAX.
