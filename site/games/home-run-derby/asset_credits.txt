PCBL Alpha 21 — asset provenance

PCBL player photographs, logos, reference footage, player identities, statistics,
outfit palettes and game files were supplied by the user / existing project.

Anatomical head and lower-body topology:
MakeHuman Community base mesh and macro targets, released under CC0 in 2020.
Modified and fitted to the existing PCBL dimensions and rig.
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md

Face diffuse: MargaretToigo, young_lightskinned_male_diffuse_Freckles.png,
from the MakeHuman skins02 CC0 pack. Render-time tint varies by player.
Face normal and specular: Mindfront, Aksel_Skin_NRM.png and Aksel_Skin_SPEC.png,
from the same CC0 pack. Normal strength is reduced for the game's style.
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Three.js r159: MIT. Full license in vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.
The anatomy and materials are artistic base assets; they are not player scans.

- Audio: blitzball-hit.ogg copied from the supplied PCBL simulator build; ambient wind is procedurally generated in-game.
