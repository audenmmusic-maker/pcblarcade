    PCBL ATTAXCLASSIC RULES

    Choose to play as the home team, away team or let the game decide randomly. Both sides receive seven random hitters and five random pitchers in a fixed, hidden order. Hitters and pitchers both rotate after every at-bat. Each pile cycles back to its first card without changing order.

    1. Roll the die: 1–2 is FB, 3–4 is BRK, and 5–6 is OFF.
    2. Both cards flip and the matching pitch ratings are compared.
    3. If the hitter is higher, use the hitter card’s result. If the pitcher is higher, the batter is out.
    4. Equal ratings are always a walk.
    5. After two outs, switch sides. Play three innings; ties continue into extras.

    One fixed standard stadium is active. Power cards are not used. In online games, the defending player rolls and advances the at-bat. PCBL Draft rosters can be carried into online play; each player keeps their own drafted 7-hitter / 5-pitcher team.
